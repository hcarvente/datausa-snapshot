How to read this data
  1. Survey estimates, not a headcount. The numbers come from the Census ACS PUMS 5-year sample. "2024" pools 2020–2024. Small groups and small industries are noisy.
  2. Average wage is a mean. High earners pull it up, and it mixes full-time, part-time and part-year workers.
  3. Gaps are not proof of cause. Group averages differ partly because of age, education, hours worked and occupation mix.
  4. A share is not representation. Compare a group's share in an industry with its share of the whole workforce (the dark tick).
  5. Census categories. Race and Hispanic origin are self-reported and asked separately. Gender is the Census male/female question. Age ranges and the grouping of small categories are choices made in this app.
  6. "Workforce" here means employed people plus armed forces, each counted in one industry.
Data: U.S. Census Bureau ACS PUMS 5-year estimates, served by Data USA.
